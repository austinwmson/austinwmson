I design and build the production systems that make creative work ship.

Most of what I do now is getting design experience into a form a language model can use, so it makes similar decisions on its own across hundreds of sessions instead of starting over every morning. Brand, product, email, web, photography, video — each one with the infrastructure that keeps it running.

### About the graph

Some of it isn't me typing. Four programs run on schedules — one keeping things in order, one going out to find work worth doing, one running research, one taking items off the backlog and finishing them. They commit under my name and escalate what they can't decide instead of guessing, which is the only reason I trust any of it.

Roughly a quarter of last year's commits came from those programs. The rest were sessions.

### Why the repos are private

Most of what I build runs against client work — brand systems, marketing infrastructure, listing data. That material isn't mine to publish. What's public here will grow as I pull the general parts out of the specific ones.
